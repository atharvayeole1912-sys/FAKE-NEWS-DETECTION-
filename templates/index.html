ABOUT THE PROJECT

Intelligent News Verification

TruthLens is a machine-learning based fake news detection system designed to help users evaluate the credibility of online news content.

⚠️ This system provides a machine-learning prediction and should not be treated as absolute proof that a news article is true or false.
